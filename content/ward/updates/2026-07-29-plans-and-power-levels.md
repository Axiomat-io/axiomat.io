Two changes worth knowing about, both live now.

## Power levels

Every assistant now runs at a power level you choose, and you can change it per assistant or per run.

- **Normal** — the default, and what nearly everything should use. Monitors, trackers, digests, feed pollers.
- **High** — a stronger model for summarizing, classifying, and routine analysis. Costs 3 runs.
- **Max** — the most capable model, for research synthesis and multi-step judgment. Costs 5 runs.

The cost is charged against the same daily budget your assistants already share, so turning one assistant up to Max just means fewer runs elsewhere that day. Nothing changes silently — an assistant stays at whatever level you set until you move it.

## Plans

Ward now has three plans — **Basic** at $9.99, **Plus** at $19.99, and **Pro** at $29.99 a month — and every one of them starts with a **14-day free trial**.

They all include the same features. The only differences are how many runs you get each day (4, 9, and 15) and whether Max mode is available; Basic runs Normal and High, which is the whole range that fits inside a 4-run day anyway.

The old free tier is gone. We'd rather be straight about why: at what the models actually cost, a permanent free tier only breaks even if roughly a third of the people using it convert to paying, which is not a thing that happens. A trial that ends is honest. A free tier we quietly starve of capability is not.

**Your daily budget is a hard cap.** When it's spent, Ward pauses until tomorrow. It never runs past your plan and bills you the difference.
