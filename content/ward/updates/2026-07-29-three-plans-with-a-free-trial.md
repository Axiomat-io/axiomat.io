Ward now has three plans — **Basic** at $9.99, **Plus** at $19.99, and **Pro** at $29.99 a month — and every one of them starts with a **14-day free trial**.

They all include the same features. The only differences are how many runs you get each day (3, 9 and 15) and whether [Max mode](/ward/updates/2026-07-25-power-levels/) is available — Basic runs Normal and High, which is the whole range that fits inside a 3-run day anyway.

**Your daily budget is a hard cap.** When it's spent, Ward pauses until tomorrow. It never runs past your plan and bills you the difference — the price on the plan is the price.
