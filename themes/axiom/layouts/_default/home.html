What it does

Hand off the little things you keep meaning to keep an eye on.

There's a whole pile of stuff you'd love to stay on top of but never quite do. Describe any of it to Ward and it builds an assistant that handles it for you — quietly, on a schedule, and only bugging you when it matters. A few of the things people set up:

⏰ Never miss a deadline

Renewals, subscriptions, warranties, the passport that expires next spring. It watches the dates and nudges you in time — not the day after.

📈 A market watch you don't babysit

Tell it the stocks or themes you follow. Each morning you get a plain-English read on what moved and why — and it can keep a running scorecard of its own calls. Paper only; it never touches your money.

📰 The news you care about, once a day

Your topics, one tidy digest — no doomscrolling, and it never shows you the same story twice.

✉️ Tame your inbox

Connect Gmail and it sorts the noise, summarizes the threads that matter, and drafts replies for you to approve before anything sends.

📅 Keep your calendar sane

Connect Google Calendar and it reminds you, preps you for what's next, and helps you get things on the books.

📓 A living digest, kept current

A research log, a trip plan, a reading list, a weekly wrap-up — kept up to date for you, and saved to Notion if that's where you live.

It's like having a few tireless little assistants who each handle one thing — and never forget, never nag, and never need managing.

How it works

You have a conversation. It does the rest.

No setup screens, no rules to configure, no code. You chat with Ward like you'd text a capable friend — and it turns what you said into an assistant that quietly gets it done.

Step 1
Just say what you want
YouKeep an eye on my stock watchlist and give me a short read each morning on what moved and why.
YouRemind me before any of my subscriptions renew, and before my passport expires.
YouEvery evening, sum up the important emails from my inbox and draft replies I can approve.

Plain English. Ward figures out what it needs and builds the assistant for you.

Step 2
It runs on its own
Works on a schedule— every morning, every hour, or when something happens. You don't have to be around.
Remembers as it goes— it knows what it already told you, so you never get the same thing twice.
Gets sharper over time— when an assistant needs something new to do its job better, Ward gives it that — you don't lift a finger.

Set it up once. It just keeps going — quietly, in the background.

Step 3
You stay in control
📋 See everything, any time
→ Each assistant gets its own simple page — the latest results, at a glance, whenever you want.
→ You can always see exactly what it looked at and what it did.
✅ Nothing happens behind your back
→ Anything that sends, posts, or changes something waits for your OK first.
→ Or keep an assistant "look, don't touch" — it can read and report, but never act.

Helpful and hands-off, but never out of your hands.

Trust & control

Helpful, but never careless with your stuff.

🙋 Nothing without your OK

Anything that sends an email, posts, or changes something waits for you to approve it — or keep an assistant read-only, so it can look and report but never act.

🔒 Your accounts stay yours

We never train AI on your data. Your passwords and app logins are handled securely and are never handed over to the assistants themselves.

Every run is written down. You can see exactly what an assistant looked at and what it did, any time — and switch it off whenever you like.
